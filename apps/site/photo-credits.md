# Photo credits

The photos in `src/assets/photos/` illustrate "your files" on the home page. They come from
[Openverse](https://openverse.org) and are all CC0 or marked public domain, so no attribution is
required; it is recorded here anyway. Pictures of identifiable people are avoided on purpose
(CC0 photos carry no model release).

Each one is cropped and compressed to a small AVIF with a WebP fallback (`avifenc -q 55`,
`magick -quality 72`): sunset 640×360, landscapes 420×280, the rest 300×300.
