Bento for Android

Your phone’s photos, in your private cloud.

Bento for Android backs up your phone’s photos and videos, encrypted, to your own Telegram account and puts your whole library in your pocket: photos, files, search and collections, just like on your computer.

Download for Android How to install

Android 10 or later. Free, no ads, no analytics. Installed from a signed APK published on GitHub; not on Google Play yet. Bento uses the Telegram API and is not affiliated with Telegram.

On your phone

Your whole library, fast and in order

Everything you save from any of your devices shows up on the phone too. Thumbnails are kept on the phone; originals download when you open them.

  • Camera roll backup. Camera and Screenshots back up on their own, on Wi-Fi only by default. You choose which other folders to include.
  • Free up space. Remove originals from the phone once they are backed up and verified. Never automatic: Android asks you to confirm.
  • Share to Bento from any app: photos, PDFs, notes or links go to the folder you pick.
  • Join with a QR code. On your computer: Settings › Devices › Add device. Scan it with the phone’s camera and confirm the four emojis.

Install

How to install it

Because Bento doesn’t come from Google Play, Android asks you once to allow installing apps from your browser.

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    On the phone, tap the download button above. When it finishes, open the file from the notification or from Downloads.

  2. 2

    Allow installing unknown apps

    If Android asks, tap Settings and turn on “Allow from this source” for your browser. It is also under Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps.

  3. 3

    Install

    Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn’t recognize the app, choose to install anyway or let it scan the app. Afterwards you can turn the permission from step 2 off again.

  4. 4

    Open Bento

    Choose “Get started” to connect your Telegram, or “I already use Bento on another device” and scan your computer’s QR code.

Came here from your computer’s QR code? After installing, scan it again: Bento opens with the code already filled in.

Updates

Updates from inside the app, never without asking

When there is a new version, Bento tells you in Settings › About and with a calm notice. You decide when to install it.

  • Bento checks a signed list of versions; if the signature isn’t valid, it ignores it.
  • Before installing, it checks the downloaded APK’s SHA-256 and that it is signed with the same certificate as the app you already have.
  • Android’s own installer does the install and asks you to confirm. It never updates silently, and your data stays.
  • You can also update by hand: download the new APK from this page and install it over the current one.
Privacy

The same protections as on your computer

Encrypted on the phone

Photos, names, dates and location are encrypted before they leave the phone. Telegram only stores data it cannot read.

No analytics, no ads

No third-party SDKs and no automatic crash reports. Bento only connects to Telegram, to the relay while pairing, and to GitHub to check for updates.

Keys on the phone

Protected by the Android Keystore and kept out of Google backups. A new phone joins by pairing.

App lock

With your fingerprint, your face or the phone’s PIN. It also hides content in the recent apps view.
