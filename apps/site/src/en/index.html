How it works

Three steps and you are done

You use the Telegram account you already have. Bento takes care of the rest in the background.

  1. 1

    Connect your Telegram

    Open Telegram on your phone › Settings › Devices › Link Desktop Device and scan the QR code Bento shows you. You can also sign in with your phone number.

  2. 2

    Choose what to back up

    Bento suggests Pictures, Documents and Desktop. Turn on the folders you want: they upload in the background, resume on their own and never block your computer.

  3. 3

    Save your recovery kit

    A code only you have. With your Telegram and that code you can recover everything, even if you lose your computers.

Your cloud lives in three private channels Bento creates in your account (“Bento · Archivos”, “Bento · Sync” and “Bento · Bandeja”) inside a chat folder called “Bento”. You are the only member.

Privacy

Your files, unreadable to everyone but you

Bento encrypts everything it uploads on your computer. Neither Telegram nor Bento can open your cloud.

Encrypted on your computer

Contents, file and folder names, dates, photo locations and the text Bento recognizes are encrypted before they leave.

What Telegram sees

That you use Bento, how much encrypted data you store, when you upload it and which devices are signed in. Not what it is or what it is called.

No Bento servers

There are no Bento accounts and no servers that store your files. To pair devices, a relay passes encrypted messages for a few minutes and forgets them.

You hold the key

Keys live on your devices and in your recovery kit. Bento cannot open your cloud or reset your access if you lose them all.

All your devices

One code and your other computer is in

Add as many computers as you like. They all see the same library and changes arrive on their own: rename, move or delete on one and the others follow.

  • On the computer you already use: Settings › Devices › Add device. You will see a QR code and a code like K7Q2-MXR9-T4WA.
  • On the new one: open Bento, choose “I already use Bento on another device” and type the code or paste the link.
  • Check that the four emojis match on both screens and press Add. If they don’t match, press Not me.
  • The code expires after 10 minutes and works once. Don’t have your other computer at hand? Sign in with Telegram and use your recovery kit.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you start

Is there really no storage limit?

Telegram does not charge for storage or announce a total limit for what you keep in its cloud, and Bento splits large files into parts, so Telegram’s per-file limit does not affect you. But it is not a contract: Telegram can change its terms, limit speed or restrict accounts at any time.

Telegram also deletes accounts that stay offline for too long (you choose the period in its privacy settings), and your cloud would go with the account. For anything irreplaceable, keep another copy as well.

What can Telegram see?

That your account has three channels created by Bento, the size and time of each encrypted part you upload or download, and your devices’ sessions (as with any Telegram app). It cannot see contents, names, the dates or locations of your photos, or your searches.

What you choose to send unencrypted is visible: copies you share to a chat and what you forward to the inbox until Bento imports it. More detail.

Is Bento an official Telegram app?

No. Bento uses the Telegram API with its own application ID and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.

What if I lose my phone?

Bento stores nothing on your phone: you only use it to sign in to Telegram. Your computers with Bento keep working. Recover your Telegram account as you normally would and, to be safe, check which sessions are open in Telegram › Settings › Devices.

What if I lose a computer?

From another of your computers, go to Settings › Devices and remove the lost one: Bento signs it out of Telegram, changes your cloud’s keys and gives you a new recovery kit. Whatever that computer had already downloaded cannot be erased remotely.

If it was your only computer, install Bento on another one, connect your Telegram and use your recovery kit.

What if I lose my recovery kit?

As long as you have a computer with Bento, create a new one in Settings › Account and cloud; the old one stops working. If you lose all your computers and the kit, nobody, not even Bento, can open your cloud again.

Can I use Bento without Telegram?

Yes. On the welcome screen choose “Use only on this computer”: your files stay on that computer, with no cloud and no sync with other devices.

Do I need Telegram Premium?

No. Bento works with free Telegram accounts.

Can I share with someone who doesn’t use Telegram?

Not yet. For now you can send a copy through Telegram to any chat. Public links, a mobile app and shared family clouds are not available yet.

Does Bento use my files to train artificial intelligence?

No. Text recognition and search run on your computer, only over your files, and nothing is used to train models. Bento does not send analytics or show ads either.
