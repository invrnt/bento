More questions and answers

Is there really no storage limit and no monthly fee?

Telegram does not charge for storage or announce a total limit for what you keep in its cloud, and Bento splits large files into parts, so Telegram’s per-file limit does not affect you. Still, we’re upfront: Telegram does not guarantee one by contract, and can change its terms, limit speed or restrict accounts at any time.

Telegram also deletes accounts that stay offline for too long (you choose the period in its privacy settings), and your cloud would go with the account. For anything truly irreplaceable, keep another copy as well.

How do I use Bento with OpenClaw, Claude Code, or another AI agent?

Install the Bento CLI on the computer or server where your agent runs and link it with bento setup or bento join. Then tell your agent to run bento guide first: every command returns structured JSON, supports --dry-run, skips duplicates, and routes deletes to the 30-day trash.

What can Telegram see?

That your account has three channels created by Bento, the size and time of each encrypted part you upload or download, and your devices’ sessions (as with any Telegram app). It cannot see contents, names, the dates or locations of your photos, or your searches.

What you choose to send unencrypted is visible: copies you share to a chat and what you forward to the inbox until Bento imports it. More detail.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes on Android (Android 10 or later, via signed APK on GitHub), with automatic camera backup, local OCR search, and biometric lock. There is no iPhone app yet: on iOS you can forward photos and files to “Bento · Bandeja” in Telegram so they enter your library encrypted.

Is Bento an official Telegram app?

No. Bento uses the Telegram API with its own application ID and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.

What if I lose a computer or phone?

From another of your devices, go to Settings › Devices and remove the lost one: Bento signs it out of Telegram, rotates your cloud’s keys and gives you a new recovery kit. Whatever that device had already downloaded cannot be erased remotely.

If it was your only device, install Bento on another one, connect your Telegram and use your recovery kit. If you only ran Telegram on a lost phone, Bento stored nothing on it.

What if I lose my recovery kit?

As long as you have a device with Bento, create a new one in Settings › Account and cloud; the old one stops working. If you lose all your devices and the kit, nobody, not even Bento, can open your cloud again.

Can I use Bento without Telegram?

Yes. On the welcome screen choose “Use only on this computer”: your files stay on that computer, with no cloud and no sync with other devices.

Do I need Telegram Premium?

No. Bento works with free Telegram accounts.

Does Bento use my files to train artificial intelligence?

No. Text recognition and search run on your device, only over your files, and nothing is used to train models. Bento does not send analytics or show ads either.
