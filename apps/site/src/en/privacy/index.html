Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short

  • Bento runs on your devices (desktop, Android, and CLI). There are no Bento accounts and none of our servers receive your files, their names, or your Telegram credentials.
  • What Bento stores in your Telegram is encrypted on your device first. Telegram sees content-free data: that you use Bento, sizes, timestamps, and your devices’ sessions.
  • The pairing relay only forwards end-to-end encrypted frames between two devices for at most 10 minutes.
  • No analytics, telemetry, ads, or cookies. We don’t sell data or use it to train artificial intelligence models.
  • Bento uses the Telegram API and is not affiliated with Telegram.

1. Who is responsible

Bento is offered by Humans Corporation S.A.S. (Humans Corporation), located in Colombia (“we”). For any privacy or data protection inquiry, write to privacy@bentocloud.app.

2. What Bento is

Bento is free open-source software available for desktop, Android, and the command line (CLI) that keeps your photo and file library on your devices and, if you connect your own Telegram account, stores an encrypted copy in private channels of that account to sync your devices. Bento runs locally on your devices: we do not receive your files, their names or metadata, your phone number, your messages, or your Telegram credentials.

3. Data that stays on your devices

  • Your library: files, thumbnails, previews, names, folders, albums, favorites, metadata (dates, camera details, location), and the local search index (OCR recognized text and smart search data if enabled).
  • Each device’s Telegram session, your cloud’s keys, and the device identity, encrypted with a key protected by the operating system’s secure store (or a protected local key file on headless servers).
  • Settings, local technical logs, and the state of background tasks. Logs never include credentials, passwords, or keys.

If you authorize an external AI agent or script (such as OpenClaw, Claude Code, or Codex) to operate the Bento CLI on your machine or server, that agent interacts locally with your library under the permissions and privacy policy of that third-party agent; Bento never sends your files to any external AI service.

You can erase all local data by uninstalling Bento and removing its data folder.

4. Data stored in your Telegram account

Telegram processes your account data under its own privacy policy as the independent provider of your account. Bento stores your files and sync log in three private channels of your account, encrypted on your device before upload.

Telegram can see the title and description of those channels, the number and size of the encrypted parts, upload and download times, your devices’ sessions (app name, model or system, and IP address), and a pinned technical message containing internal identifiers, the creation date, and an encrypted copy of your keys protected by your recovery kit. What you choose to send unencrypted (sharing via Telegram, the inbox, and pairing without the relay) is explained on the security page.

6. This website

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, trackers, or third-party assets. Cloudflare processes the IP address and technical headers of each request to serve the pages and protect the site from abuse. The device pairing page reads the code exclusively inside your browser from the URL fragment after “#”, which is never sent to any server.

7. What we don’t do

  • We don’t create user accounts, profiles, or directories.
  • We don’t include analytics, telemetry, or ads.
  • We don’t sell, rent, or share personal data.
  • We don’t use your files or data obtained from Telegram to train artificial intelligence models. OCR and search run locally on your devices.

8. Legal bases and international reach

Bento can be used from anywhere in the world. When the minimal technical data required to operate this website or the pairing relay reaches us (such as transient IP addresses needed to deliver the connection and prevent abuse), we process it on the basis of:

  • performance of the service or feature you request when visiting the website or initiating device pairing;
  • our legitimate interest in keeping the website and relay available, secure, and protected against automated abuse; and
  • compliance with applicable legal obligations, consistent with Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and, where applicable based on your place of residence, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent data protection laws.

9. Retention

  • Pairing relay: each room’s state is automatically deleted after at most 10 minutes. Invocation logs and traces are disabled; the relay only emits structured technical events containing no IP addresses or content (event name, room age, frame counts, and error codes), retained for Cloudflare Workers’ standard observability retention window (currently up to 3 days on the Free plan or up to 7 days on Paid plans), with no separate copies stored outside Cloudflare.
  • Website: network-level technical logs handled by Cloudflare are retained under Cloudflare’s retention policy.
  • On your devices and in your Telegram: kept until you delete them. The trash retains deleted items for 30 days, and the inbox removes imported messages after 24 hours (configurable).

10. Your rights

Because we do not store your library or maintain user accounts, you control your data directly: you can delete files, empty the trash, revoke devices, delete Bento’s channels in Telegram, or remove Bento’s local data folder. Rights over data processed by Telegram are exercised directly with Telegram.

To exercise your rights of access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction, or to request additional information, write to privacy@bentocloud.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with Colombia’s Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) or, if you reside outside Colombia and mandatory local law provides for it, with your local data protection authority.

11. Children

Bento is not intended for anyone below the minimum age required to use Telegram or give valid digital consent in their country.

12. Security

We explain how we encrypt your data, what each party sees, and the system’s technical boundaries on the security page.

13. Changes

If we update this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, announce them in the release notes.
