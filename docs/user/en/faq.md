# Frequently asked questions

## Is there really no storage limit?

Telegram does not charge for storage or announce a total limit for what you keep in its cloud, and Bento splits large files into parts, so Telegram’s per-file limit doesn’t affect you. But **it is not a contract**: Telegram can change its terms, limit speed or restrict accounts at any time, and Bento cannot prevent that.

Telegram also deletes accounts that stay offline for too long (you choose the period in Telegram’s privacy settings), and your cloud would go with the account. For anything you cannot afford to lose, keep another copy as well.

## What can Telegram see?

That your account has three channels created by Bento, the size and time of each encrypted part you upload or download, and your computers’ sessions, as with any Telegram app. It **cannot** see the contents of your files, their names, the dates or locations of your photos, your folders or your searches.

It does see what you choose to send unencrypted: copies you share to a chat and what you forward to the inbox until Bento imports it. Details in [Privacy and security](privacy-and-security.md).

## Is Bento an official Telegram app?

No. Bento uses the Telegram API with its own application ID and **is not affiliated with Telegram** or endorsed by Telegram.

## Can I use Bento without Telegram?

Yes. On the welcome screen choose **Use only on this computer**: your library stays on that computer, with no cloud, no sync with other devices and no recovery kit.

## What if I lose my phone?

Bento stores nothing on your phone: you only use it to sign in to Telegram. Your computers with Bento keep working. Recover your Telegram account as you normally would and check which sessions are open in Telegram › Settings › Devices.

## What if I lose a computer?

From another of your computers, in Settings › Devices, remove the one you lost: Bento signs it out of Telegram, changes your cloud’s keys and gives you a new recovery kit. Whatever that computer had already downloaded cannot be erased remotely. If it was your only computer, install Bento on another one, connect your Telegram and use your kit. More in [Add a device](add-a-device.md#remove-a-device).

## What if I lose my recovery kit?

As long as you have a computer with Bento, create a new one in Settings › Account and cloud; the old one stops working. If you lose **all your computers and the kit too**, nobody, not even Bento, can open your cloud again. More in [Recovery kit](recovery-kit.md).

## Do I need Telegram Premium?

No. Bento works with free Telegram accounts.

## Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. On your phone you use Telegram to connect your account and to send things to the [inbox](telegram-inbox.md).

## Can I share with someone who doesn’t use Telegram?

Not yet. For now you can [send a copy through Telegram](share-and-import.md) to any chat. Public links and shared family clouds are not available yet.

## Can I see my files from Telegram?

No. Bento’s channels only contain encrypted data with meaningless names. Use Bento to see your files.

## What happens if I delete Bento’s channels in Telegram?

You delete your cloud. Bento keeps your files there, along with the information it uses to sync your computers. Don’t delete, edit or leave “Bento · Archivos” or “Bento · Sync”.

## How long does the first copy take?

It depends on how much you have, your connection and Telegram. With many files it can take hours or days. Bento works in the background and picks up where it left off.

## Does it work offline?

Partly: you can browse your library, search and open what is already on the computer. New things upload and sync when the connection is back.

## Does Bento use my files to train artificial intelligence?

No. Text recognition and search run on your computer, only over your files, and nothing is used to train models. Bento does not send analytics or show ads either.

## What happens if I uninstall Bento?

Your cloud stays in your Telegram and your other computers keep working. That computer’s data folder is not deleted automatically. If you are done with that computer for good, remove it from another one to sign it out of Telegram and change the keys.
