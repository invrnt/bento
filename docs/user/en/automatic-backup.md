# Automatic backup

Choose folders on your computer and Bento uploads everything in them, plus anything you add later, to your cloud, encrypted. It works in the background, retries on its own and picks up where it left off if you shut down or sleep the computer.

## Choose folders

- On the first run, Bento suggests folders that already exist on your computer, such as Pictures, Documents, Desktop or Downloads. Turn them on with one click.
- Later, in Settings › Backup, you can turn folders on or off, remove them and add others with **Add folder…**.
- For safety, Bento does not accept the root of a drive, Bento’s own data folder, or repeated or nested folders.

Backup **copies**: your original files stay where they were. Removing a folder from backup does not delete anything already uploaded.

## See progress

The sidebar footer and **Activity** show what is uploading, how much is left and whether anything needs your attention. If a file fails, it appears in Activity with **Retry**.

## Pause and limit

In Settings › Backup, or from the Bento icon in the system tray:

- **Pause backup** and **Resume backup** whenever you like.
- **Pause on battery:** on by default. On laptops, backup waits until you plug in the charger.
- **Speed limit:** so Bento doesn’t take your whole connection.

## How long it takes

The first copy can take hours or days if you have many files: it depends on your connection and on Telegram. Bento uploads one file at a time so it doesn’t overload your computer or your account, and if Telegram asks it to wait, it waits and carries on by itself. Large files are split into parts, so there is no maximum size per file.

## Good to know

- What gets uploaded appears in your library and on all your computers.
- Bento needs to be open (even in the background) to back up. That is why it opens when you sign in to your computer.
- If you use Bento only on this computer, without Telegram, there is no cloud backup.
