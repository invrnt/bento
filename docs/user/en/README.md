# Bento guides

Bento is your private cloud: it keeps your photos and files on your computer and, if you connect your Telegram account, an encrypted copy in your own Telegram that keeps all your devices the same.

Español: [Guías de Bento](../es/README.md).

> The app is Spanish-first. Button and menu names in these guides are the English wording; if your Bento is in Spanish, the [Spanish guides](../es/README.md) use the exact labels.

- [Connect your Telegram](connect-telegram.md): with a QR code or your phone number.
- [Add a device](add-a-device.md): your library on another computer with one code.
- [Recovery kit](recovery-kit.md): the spare key to your cloud.
