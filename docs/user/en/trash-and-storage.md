# Trash and space on this computer

## Trash

- When you delete something, Bento moves it to the trash and shows **Moved to trash · Undo**. Press **Undo** to get it back right away.
- Deleted items stay in the trash for **30 days**. From there you can restore them, delete them forever or empty the trash.
- After 30 days, Bento deletes them forever automatically.
- The trash is the same on all your computers: what you delete on one goes to the trash on all of them, and what you restore comes back on all of them.

**Delete forever** removes the file from your cloud in Telegram and from all your computers, and cannot be undone. If another computer had already saved that file outside Bento (for example, you exported it to another folder), that copy is not deleted.

## Space on this computer

Bento doesn’t need your whole cloud on every computer:

- **Thumbnails are always on the computer**, so your library shows up instantly, even offline.
- **Originals download when you open them** and are kept in a cache of up to 10 GB per computer. When it fills up, Bento frees what you haven’t opened for the longest time first.
- Videos start playing without waiting for the whole file to download.
- You can change the cache size in Settings.

Offline, you can browse your library and open whatever is already in the cache. Everything else opens when the connection is back.

[Automatic backup](automatic-backup.md) copies your folders to the cloud; the original files stay where they are.

## How much space your cloud uses

The sidebar footer and Settings › Account and cloud show how much space your cloud uses and how many files it has. That space is in your Telegram, not on your computer.
