# Recovery kit

The recovery kit is the spare key to your cloud. With your Telegram account and this code you can recover everything, even if you lose all your computers.

## What it is

- A 36-character code in 9 groups of 4, for example `K7QM-3XRA-…`.
- The “Kit de recuperación de Bento” PDF also includes your account name, the date you created your cloud, the code and a QR code of it.
- It has a checksum: if you mistype a character, Bento says “check the code” instead of failing silently. You can type it in lowercase, with or without dashes; O and 0, and I, L and 1, are treated the same.

## Save it

When it creates your cloud, Bento shows you the code. You can:

- **Save PDF** or **Print** it, and keep the paper somewhere safe.
- **Copy** the code into a password manager.

To confirm you saved it, type its last group. If you press **Later**, Bento keeps reminding you in Activity until you save it.

> Keep it **away from your computers**. If the kit only lives on the computer you lose, it won’t help you.

## Who can use it

Anyone who has **both** your Telegram account and this code can open your cloud. Treat it like your house key: don’t share it, don’t send it by chat and don’t upload it to Bento itself.

Bento does not store the code on your computers or on any server. Nobody, not even Bento, can recover it for you.

## Check it or create a new one

In Settings › Account and cloud › Recovery kit you can:

- **Verify** that the code you saved is the right one.
- **Create a new one**, for example if you think someone has seen it. The old one stops working immediately.

Bento also creates a new kit, mandatorily, every time you [remove a device](add-a-device.md#remove-a-device), because it changes your cloud’s keys.

## Use it

On a new computer: **I already use Bento on another device** › **I don’t have the other device** › connect your Telegram › type the code. Bento opens your cloud and downloads your library.

## What happens if…

- **You lose the kit but have a computer with Bento:** create a new one from that computer. You have lost nothing.
- **You lose a computer but have the kit:** install Bento on another one, connect your Telegram and use the kit. Then [remove the lost computer](add-a-device.md#remove-a-device).
- **You lose all your computers and the kit too:** nobody can open your cloud again. The data is still in your Telegram, but encrypted, with no way to decrypt it.
